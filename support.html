Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

If you downloaded the app before version 1.3 (October 2026): yes, everything stays free for you, including after updates. Families who are new to the app from version 1.3 get a free 7-day week, then choose Monthly (£2.49), Yearly (£19.99) or a one-off Lifetime purchase (£39.99). A grown-up starts the free week behind the parental gate, and you can cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Prices may change in the future; Apple tells you first. See the Terms of Use.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel?

To restore: open the paywall from the “Free week” or “Unlock everything” button on the Home screen (behind the parental gate) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought the plan. To cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Draw & Learn. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the free week or the current period ends means you are not charged again.

Is my child's data safe?

Draw & Learn does not collect personal data, show advertising, or track children. Drawings remain on the device unless you choose to export them. See our Privacy Policy.

What ages is it designed for?

Draw & Learn (listed on the App Store as “Draw & Learn to Talk with Bob”) is made for children aged 5 and under, with guided drawing, tracing, colouring, first words, phonics, shapes, and numbers. Parent settings and links sit behind a parental gate.

Does the app use the internet, microphone, or camera?

No. Everything children use works fully offline. Bob’s voice is either audio bundled with the app or Apple’s on-device text-to-speech. The app never asks for microphone or camera access. The only permission it may request is to add a finished drawing to Photos when you choose to save it.

The app is not working correctly

  • Confirm the device runs iOS or iPadOS 16.6 or later.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Restart the device.
  • If the issue continues, email us with the device model and a description.
